Welcome to EcoGuard

Protecting Nature Through Technology. EcoGuard is an environmental monitoring system designed to analyze forest vegetation, monitor green coverage, identify environmental risks and support sustainable environmental protection using satellite data and machine learning.

What Does EcoGuard Do?

EcoGuard combines satellite-based environmental analysis and intelligent risk prediction to help people understand forest conditions and take informed environmental actions.

🛰️

Satellite-Based Monitoring

Uses satellite imagery to observe vegetation conditions across selected geographical areas. Click to explore satellite monitoring.

🌳

Green Coverage Analysis

Estimates vegetation coverage using NDVI and compares it with the project's green coverage target. Click to explore green coverage analysis.

🤖

Environmental Risk Prediction

Applies machine learning and environmental indicators to estimate potential vegetation-related risks.

📊

Deforestation Change Detection

Compares current and previous satellite observations to identify possible vegetation changes.

📧

Email Risk Alerts

Sends email notifications when the analysis identifies a configured environmental risk level.

🌱

Tree Plantation Suggestions

Provides approximate tree plantation planning suggestions based on estimated green coverage gaps.
